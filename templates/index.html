The Index

What is the Human Development Index?

The HDI is a summary measure published by the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) that ranks countries by more than just income. It condenses three dimensions of human wellbeing into a single score between 0 and 1, making it possible to compare long-term progress across very different economies.

Health Life expectancy at birth
Education Mean and expected years of schooling
Income Gross National Income (GNI) per capita
Gender Gender Development & Inequality Indices
Planet CO₂ emissions per capita

The Stakes

Why HDI matters

GDP alone can hide how ordinary people actually live — a resource-rich country can be wealthy on paper while its citizens have short lifespans or little access to education. HDI corrects for this by rewarding countries that convert income into longer, healthier, more educated lives.

This predictor extends the standard four-feature model with Gender Development Index (GDI), Gender Inequality Index (GII), and CO₂ per capita — giving a richer, more complete picture of development. The model is auto-selected from three algorithms using 5-fold cross-validation.

Built on Seven Signals

What the model looks at

Each prediction is built from seven indicators spanning health, education, income, gender equity, and environmental footprint — feeding the best-performing model from a 5-fold CV comparison.

01

Life Expectancy

Average number of years a newborn is expected to live, given current mortality patterns. A proxy for healthcare quality and living conditions.

02

Mean Years of Schooling

The average number of years of education completed by adults aged 25 and older — a backward-looking measure of educational attainment.

03

Expected Years of Schooling

The years of schooling a child entering school today can expect to receive — a forward-looking measure of access to education.

04

GNI per Capita

Gross National Income per person, adjusted for purchasing power — a measure of the average material standard of living.

05

Gender Dev. Index

Ratio of female to male HDI. A value of 1.0 indicates perfect gender equality; values below 1.0 indicate female disadvantage.

06

Gender Ineq. Index

Measures loss of achievement due to gender inequality in reproductive health, empowerment, and labour market participation. Lower is better.

07

CO₂ per Capita

Carbon dioxide emissions per person from fossil fuel production (tonnes). Adds an environmental sustainability dimension to the prediction.

Under the Hood

How a prediction is made

The same pipeline used to train the model runs — in miniature — every time you submit the form below.

1

Wrangle the dataset

The wide-format UNDP CSV (191 countries × 1990–2021) is melted into long format — one row per country-year — yielding thousands of training samples.

2

Select 7 features

Seven indicator columns are extracted per year: life expectancy, mean and expected schooling, GNI, GDI, GII, and CO₂. Rows with any missing value are dropped.

3

Scale features

A StandardScaler normalizes each indicator so no single feature — like GNI, which spans a much wider range than schooling — dominates the model.

4

Compare & select model

Linear Regression, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting are each evaluated with 5-fold CV. The algorithm achieving the lowest RMSE is selected and retrained on all training data.

5

Predict and classify

Your submitted values are scaled with the same fitted scaler, passed through the trained model, and the resulting score is mapped to a UNDP development category.
